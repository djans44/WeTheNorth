{# The crests, as shields. A shield is a field in its category's colour, a dark edge, and a charge in parchment. There are seven colours and twenty-eight charges, and between them they tell one crest from another without a word of text -- which is what a wall of them on a manager's page needs. Held crests are deliberately not here. Those seven ring a manager's sigil instead, in gold, silver or bronze, and keeping the two languages apart is what stops a title being mistaken for a trophy. {% from "crestart.html" import crestshield %} {{ crestshield(c.code, c.category) }} Colour, the parchment stroke and the display face are all classes rather than attributes: an SVG presentation attribute cannot carry a var(), and these have to come from the tokens like everything else on the site. The charges were drawn on a 20x20 grid and are placed at translate(10,13). fill-rule evenodd is set on the group, so a hole is drawn by putting one shape inside another -- the ring on Denied the Throne, the pips on the die, the slits in the broom. #} {% macro crestshield(code, category, size=56) -%} {%- endmacro %}
